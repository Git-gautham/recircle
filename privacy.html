Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

What we collect

We collect your email, display name, role, area, listing details and photos, approximate pickup locations, and chat messages.

Why we collect it

We use this information to operate the reuse marketplace, connect people, and provide messaging. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Account and marketplace data is stored with Supabase. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Who can see it

Listings and display names are public. Chats are visible only to the two participants. Do not share exact home addresses in a listing.

Deleting your data

Use Profile → Delete my account to remove your account, profile, listings and photos. Messages you already sent stay visible to the person you were chatting with, shown as from a “Former member”, so they keep a record of the hand-off. Completed exchanges stay in the community totals without your name. Contact the project owner for other requests.
