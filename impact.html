    How this is calculated: estimated CO₂e avoided = exchanged material weight (kg) × the category factor in js/data.js. Factors are rounded, indicative estimates for reuse instead of disposal — not independently verified carbon-accounting results. Current factors are: paper/cardboard 0.9, plastic 1.5, e-waste 2.0, metal 4.0, glass 0.3, textiles 3.0, wood 0.6, organic 0.5, construction 0.05, and other 0.5 kg CO₂e per kg. The dashboard counts only completed exchanges; the estimate shown while creating or viewing a listing is potential impact until the exchange is confirmed. Equivalents use common rule-of-thumb values — ~21 kg CO₂ absorbed per tree per year, ~0.17 kg CO₂ per km in a petrol car, and ~0.008 kg CO₂ per smartphone charge.
